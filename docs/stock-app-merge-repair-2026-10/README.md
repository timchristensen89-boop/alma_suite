# Repair of the 17 Oct app duplicate merges (prepared 17 Oct 2026, **not applied**)

Between 06:48 and 06:50 AEDT on 17 Oct, 27 duplicate groups (29 records) were merged in the app (Items → Duplicates), and Agave Syrup was edited. The app's merge does two things that our scripted merges deliberately avoid:

- **It adds the two records' holdings together.** On each venue's 1 Oct sheet, staff had usually counted only one of the two records. The other record's holding was a leftover from an August or September count. Adding them double counts.
- **It adds two lines on the same sheet together and deletes one.** It did this on applied sheets too, so the recorded history changed. Where units differed, the result is wrong: a counted 12.5 kg flour bag now reads as 1 kg of flour on the 1 Oct sheet.

Every original row is still in a production copy taken at 06:37, before the merges. `repair-data.json` is generated by comparing that copy with production at 12:39. No item changed in between apart from the 06:48–06:50 edits.

## What the repair does

1. **History, exactly as recorded.** Puts back the 53 deleted lines and restores the 18 lines that were added together. It also points the 27 affected stock movements back at their own lines. Lines stay on the surviving record, the same repoint-only rule as every scripted merge. After a rehearsal apply, every stocktake line and movement link is identical to the 06:37 copy.

   | Sheet | Lines put back or restored |
   |---|---:|
   | Stocktake 10/1/2026 (Alma Avalon, applied) | 26 |
   | Alma Avalon — Bar & FOH 9/1/2026 (applied) | 11 |
   | St Alma — Kitchen 31/8/2026 (applied) | 13 |
   | St Alma — Bar & FOH 31/08/2026 (applied) | 3 |
   | Stocktake 9/1/2026 (phantom "Avalon", never applied) | 17 |
   | VOID stray count (never applied) | 1 |

2. **Holdings, one physical stock each.** For each surviving record and venue, the holding becomes the pre-merge holding of the record that was **counted on that venue's latest sheet**, converted to the surviving record's unit. The record that was not counted there is dropped as a leftover. Each change writes a stocktake-correction movement. There are 27 changes.
   - **Both counted on the same sheet, so both kept:** Lime Juice at St Alma (1 × 5 L container plus 3.8 L open) and Onion Powder at Avalon (1 + 0.5 jars). Two lines with two counts on one sheet look like separate stock. They are worth a glance on the shelf.
   - **Neither counted on the latest sheet, so the main record was kept:** Rice Basmati, white vinegar and white wine vinegar at Avalon.
   - **Unit conversions:** the 12.5 kg flour bag becomes 12.5 kg, and the 10 kg and 15 kg mayonnaise tubs become kg. Shredded mozzarella in kg becomes 2 kg bags, 7 L of pomace oil becomes 4 L tins, and 2 kg of table salt becomes 14 kg cartons. Juice containers become litres at 5 L each.

3. **Records.**
   - **Agave Syrup** goes back to the kitchen's 4 L tub at $56.73: counted per tub, 4,000 mL per tub. At 06:50 it was saved as "gram" with 750 per purchase unit, which made its cost $0.08. The evidence for the tub: its archived twin is "Agave Syrup Clear (4L)" at $56.70, every count since August is in tubs valued at $56.73, and the recipes use it by the mL. The 750 matches the bar's Crawleys Bartender Agave Syrup 750 mL (Paramount Liquor order, 26 Jul), which is a different product with no stock record of its own.
   - **Lime Juice (1 x 5L)** and **Watermelon Juice (1 x 5L)** stay counted in litres. Their purchase unit becomes the 5 L container, as supplied by Nectar Cold Pressed: Lime Juice 5L $70.00 and Pure Watermelon Juice 5L $34.80, on the 31 Aug and 6 Oct orders. The merge had set "5000 mL per litre" on them. Their cost per litre is corrected in PR #324: $14.00 and $6.96.
   - **White vinegar 4 L** loses the $1.08 placeholder purchase price the merge copied onto it.

## Safety

- **Dry run by default.** Every row is checked against what was reviewed. Any difference refuses the whole run and writes nothing. This was tested by changing one holding on a copy.
- **Backup.** `--apply` snapshots every touched row into `_stock_app_merge_repair_backup` in the same transaction.
- **Rollback.** `--rollback <runId>` restores lines, movements, holdings and records. It refuses if stock has moved on these items since, unless `--force`. In the rehearsal, rollback was byte-identical across every StocktakeLine, VenueStockItem, StockItem and InventoryMovement row.
- **Production guard.** Writing to production needs `--i-mean-production`.

```
DATABASE_URL=… tsx apps/stock-api/scripts/repair-app-merges-2026-10.ts                       # dry run
DATABASE_URL=… tsx apps/stock-api/scripts/repair-app-merges-2026-10.ts --apply --i-mean-production --run-id prod-merge-repair-YYYYMMDD
DATABASE_URL=… tsx apps/stock-api/scripts/repair-app-merges-2026-10.ts --rollback prod-merge-repair-YYYYMMDD --i-mean-production
```

## To stop it happening again (follow-up, not in this PR)

The Items → Duplicates merge in the app should stop adding holdings and same-sheet lines together. It should do what the scripted merges do: repoint history, and ask which record's holding is the real one.
